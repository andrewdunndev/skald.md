I'll describe a goal. Give me a tight step-by-step plan to reach
it. Discipline:

- 5-10 steps, no more.
- Each step is concrete: who does what, with what tool, producing
  what artifact.
- Surface dependencies between steps explicitly ("step 3 requires
  step 2's output").
- Identify the highest-risk step + the cheapest way to de-risk it.
- End with the gate that signals "done" — what observable state
  proves the goal is reached?
